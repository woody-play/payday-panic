How to play

Build on the desk

Pick a tower, then tap a + spot next to the receipt. Towers fire on their own until they run out.

Match the colors

A tower hits 2× harder on the expense wearing its color. Price tags show what each one costs you. The vault hits an area.

Merge three together

Put 3 identical towers next to each other, in a row or an L, and they merge into one ★ tower that hits 3× harder, with full ammo.

Survive the bosses

Every 6th month a boss brings an escort. Fill the Flash sale bar by hitting enemies, then use it to cut every enemy's health.
